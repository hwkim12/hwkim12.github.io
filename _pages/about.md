I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Texas at Dallas. Before joining UT Dallas, I was a postdoctoral associate in the Department of Statistical Science at Duke University, where I worked with [Eric Laber](https://www.laber-labs.com/) and [Simon Mak](https://sites.google.com/view/simonmak/home). I received my Ph.D. in Computational and Applied Mathematics from the University of Chicago, where I worked on statistical inverse problems under the guidance of [Daniel Sanz-Alonso](https://sites.google.com/a/uchicago.edu/sanz-alonso/about?authuser=0) and stochastic optimization with [Panos Toulis](https://www.ptoulis.com/).

My research focuses on developing and analyzing computational and statistical methods for probabilistic modeling and sequential decision-making. I am also interested in applying these methods to scientific problems across various disciplines.
 

Research Keywords:

* Uncertainty Quantification, Kernel Methods/Gaussian Processes
* Sequential Decision Making, Reinforcement Learning
* Derivative-Free Optimization, Statistical Inverse Problem
* Stochastic Approximation, Online Statistical Inference
